Write the BODY of a cover letter. Plain prose. No headings, no markdown,
no bullets, no signature block. Start with "Dear Hiring Manager,".

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
FORMAT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Exactly 3 paragraphs, under 250 words.

Paragraph 1 — OPENING
- First sentence: "My name is {CANDIDATE_NAME}, and I am writing to apply
  for the {JOB_TITLE} role at {COMPANY}."
- If COMPANY is "(not provided)" or empty, write "at your company" — never
  write "null".
- Then 1-2 sentences connecting the candidate to this role.

Paragraph 2 — EVIDENCE
- Mention 1-2 projects from TOP PROJECTS.
- REWRITE in first person. Do NOT quote raw bullet text.
  BAD: "ProjectName: Built X using Y."
  GOOD: "I built ProjectName, where I designed X using Y."
- Reference 2-3 matched skills, in first person, with their evidence.
- Include a concrete number if the evidence has one.

Paragraph 3 — CLOSE
- Express interest, thank the reader, invite a next step.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
HONESTY RULES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
- Use ONLY facts shown below. Never invent.
- Never claim a MISSING skill.
- Never apologise for a missing skill.
- Never claim a degree unless it's in EDUCATION.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
BANNED PHRASES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
passionate, leverage, synergy, dynamic, thrive, "eager to grow",
"innovative culture", "strong analytical mindset", "rapid learning",
"track record", "I am excited to", "I am thrilled", "hit the ground
running", "team player".

Tone: confident, specific, plain English.
